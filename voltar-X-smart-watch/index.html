What this prototype is

Interactive mock of the Voltar X watch. Hardware idea: Huawei Watch D–style mini pump + inflatable strap, iOS-simple face, one long button on the left, almost no edges. It is not a medical device — it shows how the product should feel.

Hardware you are looking at

  • Case Soft squircle. Slightly thicker than a normal watch so a coin-size pump can sit inside.
  • Button Only control. Short press = next face. Long press (in a real unit) = start BP or confirm a pill.
  • Strap Looks like a normal band. Inside is an airbag. That is the cuff. The glass does not inflate.
  • Back Optical sensor for heart rate, HRV, SpO2, skin temperature.

How blood pressure works here

Once a day (or when the user / doctor asks): watch reminds → user stays still, wrist at heart level → pump fills the strap → sensor reads pulse waves while it deflates → systolic / diastolic saved to the Voltar account → assigned doctor can see it.

Also on the watch (not a separate face yet)

  • HRV → stress estimate
  • Blood oxygen
  • Wrist skin temperature (trend, not a clinic thermometer)
  • High / low HR and BP alerts
  • Auto-sync to the subscriber’s Voltar account

What it will not do

  • Detect headaches
  • Diagnose or prescribe — the doctor in the app does that
  • Replace an upper-arm cuff when a clinical decision is needed

How to use this file

Press the long silver button, tap the screen, or use the pills under the watch. Mark taken on the meds face logs a dose in this demo only.

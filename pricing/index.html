4. Storage

  • Cloudflare R2 — live media (images, voice, video). 10 GB to start. Official R2 storage is far cheaper than $0.50/GB (about $0.015/GB-month). Your $0.50/GB figure is a safe buffer if we ever leave R2 or add transfer fees.
  • Local VPS disk (200 GB) — private backup of the same files. Not the public CDN.
  • Video consults will eat storage first. Keep a delete-after-X-days rule for raw calls unless the case is under review.

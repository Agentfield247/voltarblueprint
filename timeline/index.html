Development timeline

2–4 months to a stable MVP. Team: 2 Flutter, 1 PHP. Watch hardware is after the app.

WEEKS 1–2 · MONTH 0

Rails

  • Flutter shell (iOS / Android first).
  • VPS: Nginx, PHP API, MySQL, SMTP.
  • Figma: home = chat, shop, consult thread.
  • AI policy locked: no diagnosis, no prescription.
  • Guest session with no account.
  • Payments in test mode.

Exit: guest opens the app and sees “How are you feeling today?”

WEEKS 3–6 · MONTH 1

The habit

  • Guest chat + Voltar can explain the app.
  • Intake a doctor can read.
  • Emergency banner on danger phrases.
  • ElevenLabs one automated check-in call (same rules as text).
  • Optional Google / Apple / email in the corner.

Do not build: doctor grid, watch sync, video.

Exit: a stranger has a real conversation without signing up.

WEEKS 7–10 · MONTH 2

Money + a human

  • Quick Consult: price first → pay → assign one clinician.
  • Timed chat session.
  • After Rx pay or “save history” → 10-second signup.
  • Ugly doctor console: intake, reply, write Rx.
  • Ratings when the session ends.

Exit: guest pays, talks to a person, can rate.

WEEKS 11–14 · MONTH 3

Pharmacy + voice

  • Shop search. No stock counts. Ask availability.
  • OTC guest checkout, then prompt an account.
  • Rx-only locked until a Voltar doctor prescribed it.
  • Upload paper Rx → extract → human confirm → availability.
  • Voice (video only if the same SDK is cheap).
  • Recording consent. Files on R2 + VPS copy.
  • History: upcoming / past / cancel.

Exit: consult kit becomes a sourced drug or nearby pickup.

WEEKS 15–16 · MONTH 4

Harden

  • Thin Flutter web/desktop for video + chat.
  • Settings: allergies, meds, conditions.
  • Med reminders in the app (watch later).
  • Crash, pay, privacy, AI refusal audit.
  • Closed beta with 2–3 clinicians.

MVP exit: guest chat → paid consult → Rx → availability → pay → account. AI never plays doctor.

After month 4

  • Voltar X Series 1 (pump + cuff).
  • Daily BP + clinician flags.
  • Physical booking.
  • HMO pilots.
  • Reputation visible only after you used that doctor.
